Workspace

Add a support team

Enter a team name, what it handles, its official contact details, and one example request that belongs to it. The example is needed to include the new team in model training.

Support team contact and instructions

Keep team information accurate. Customers will see these details in their recommendation.

Add a training example

Enter a typical customer message and choose the team that actually handles it. Do not include real customer names, account numbers, passwords, or other private information.

Saved training examples

Edit the request text or correct its assigned team. Deleting a team also deletes its saved examples.

Requests needing human review

Choose the team that should handle each request. Verified decisions can be used to retrain the model.
